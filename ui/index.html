How trust is decided

  • Whitelist: a customer who has bought before and was verified. Orders within its auto-approval limit are placed without a person.
  • New customer: every order goes to the review queue. Approving it can also verify the customer.
  • Business ID: checked with the Y-tunnus checksum today. Next step: look it up in the Finnish Trade Register (PRH open data). That's more reliable than scraping personal data from the web, and GDPR-friendly.
  • Hard rules (allergens) are never overridden, even for trusted customers.
